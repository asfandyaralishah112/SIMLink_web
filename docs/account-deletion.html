Delete your Phone Link account

Phone Link — com.LabNexus.phonelink

Delete it from the app

The quickest way, and it takes effect immediately:

  1. Open Phone Link.
  2. Go to Settings → Relay Identity.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Or request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email asfandyaralishah@rocketmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Phone Link account. Requests are actioned within 30 days. We use the sending address to identify the account, so please write from that address.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and email address Deleted permanently, straight away
The list of Bluetooth headsets authorized for your account Deleted permanently, straight away
Sign-in session stored on your phone Cleared when the account is deleted, or when you uninstall the app

What was never stored

Your call log, contacts, phone numbers and call audio are used only on your own device, to show the dialer and to relay audio between your phones. None of it is ever uploaded, so there is nothing of that kind to delete.

After deletion

SIM relay stops working on every device signed in with that account, and the dialer keeps working normally. Deletion cannot be undone, but you can sign in again at any time to create a new account; its authorized headsets would need to be set up again.
