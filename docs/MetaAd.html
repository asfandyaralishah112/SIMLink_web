How it works

A short setup, then it runs in the background.

  1. Install it on both phonesThe same app goes on the phone you carry and the phone that keeps the other SIM.
  2. Sign in with one Google accountThat's what pairs the two phones to each other, and nothing else.
  3. Let them find each otherThe phones link up directly over Bluetooth. No internet needed once paired.
  4. Pair a headset with each phoneCall audio reaches you through a matched pair of Bluetooth headsets. We supply them — message us and we'll sort you out.
  5. Leave the second phone anywhere nearbyDrawer, bag, desk. Its calls come to the phone you're using.

Questions

If yours isn't here, just message us.

Do I need both phones with me?

They need to be near each other — the two phones talk over a direct Bluetooth link. Same room, same bag, same desk is fine.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The link between the phones is Bluetooth. Internet is only used the first time, to sign in.

What does it cost?

There is no subscription and no per-minute charge — calls are ordinary calls on your own SIM and your own plan. The one cost is the pair of headsets that carries the call audio between your phones. You buy those once, from us. Message us for the current price.

Why do I need the headsets?

They are how the call audio gets from one phone to the other. Ordinary headphones will not do it — the pair has to be matched and set up for this, which is why we supply them rather than pointing you at a shop.

Is my call audio sent anywhere?

No. Audio stays between your two phones. Contacts and call history are read on the phone and never uploaded.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones. Message us with the two models you have and we'll tell you straight away whether they'll work.
