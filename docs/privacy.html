Privacy Policy

Phone Link — com.LabNexus.phonelink
Last updated: 27 August 2026

Phone Link is a dialer that can relay calls between two of your own Android phones. This policy explains what the app collects, what it deliberately does not, and how to delete what it holds.

What we collect

Only what an account needs. Signing in is optional — the dialer works without it — and is required only for the SIM relay feature.

DataWhyShared?
Email address of the Google account you sign in with Identifies your account and determines which Bluetooth headsets are authorized for it No
Account identifier created when you first sign in Identifies your account on our server No

That is the complete list. There is no analytics, no advertising, no advertising ID, and no tracking of any kind in this app.

What stays on your phone

Phone Link uses several sensitive permissions. The data behind them is used on the device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

DataUsed for
Call logShowing recent calls, and recording calls made through the paired phone so your history is complete
ContactsShowing the caller's name instead of a bare number
Phone numbers and SIM detailsChoosing which SIM places a call
Microphone audioRelaying live call audio between your two phones. It is never recorded, stored or transmitted to a server
Bluetooth device addressesChecking whether a connected headset is authorized for your account
Approximate location permission (Android 11 and older only)Required by those Android versions to scan for Bluetooth devices. Your location is never read, used or collected

Data shared between your two phones

When you pair two phones, each sends the other a short identifier derived from your account so that only devices signed into the same account will link. This travels directly between your phones over Bluetooth and does not pass through us.

Who processes your data

  • Google — handles the sign-in itself. We receive your email address from it; we never see your Google password.
  • Supabase — hosts the database holding your email address, account identifier, and the list of headsets authorized for your account.

These are service providers acting on our behalf. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Security and retention

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Your account is kept until you delete it. Deleting it removes the data immediately.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Relay Identity → Delete account. Or see the account deletion page, which also covers requesting deletion by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Signing out clears the session on that phone but keeps your account. Deleting the account removes your email address, your account identifier, and the list of headsets authorized for it.

Children

Phone Link is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
